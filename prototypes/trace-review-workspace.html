Source Trace review workspace

The question this prototype answers

Can one review workspace make the complete Source Trace understandable, support deterministic termination search, and show asynchronous Resolution Proposals without implying that AI has applied a change?

Prototype for wayfinder ticket #83. Everything below is simulated in the page. Time does not pass on its own: the provider only moves when you press a provider button. Start with a walkthrough under the workspace, or open the free-play drawer at the bottom.

Sample traces are derived from the redacted trace workbook fixtures in this repository (trace_fiber.xlsx, trace_copper.xlsx, sheet "Trace From To"). Device, panel and cassette names are the redacted names from those files.
